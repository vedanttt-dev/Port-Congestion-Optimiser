# Problem Statement

## Background

Container ports worldwide face increasing congestion due to growing trade volumes, unpredictable vessel arrivals, and limited berth/crane capacity. Port operations managers must make critical decisions about berth allocation, crane scheduling, and vessel rerouting — often with incomplete information and under time pressure.

## The Problem

Port terminals experience an average of 20–40% excess wait time for vessels during peak congestion periods, costing shipping lines $50,000–$200,000 per vessel in demurrage fees. Operations teams rely on manual spreadsheets and first-come-first-served (FCFS) scheduling, which fails to optimise berth utilization, crane allocation, or detect congestion hotspots before they cascade into delays.

## Who is Affected

- **Port Operations Managers** — Responsible for berth scheduling, crane deployment, and shift planning across 24/7 operations
- **Terminal Planners** — Need to plan 72-hour ahead workforce shifts based on expected vessel arrivals
- **Shipping Lines** — Suffer demurrage costs when vessels wait at anchorage instead of berthing immediately

## Why It Matters

- Global port congestion costs the shipping industry **$10B+ annually** in demurrage and detention fees
- Suboptimal berth allocation wastes 15–30% of available crane capacity
- Manual planning takes 45+ minutes per scenario; managers cannot evaluate alternatives quickly
- Lack of forward-looking congestion prediction means problems are only identified after they occur

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Current port management tools are either:
- **Expensive enterprise TOS systems** (Terminal Operating Systems) costing $500K+ with lengthy implementation cycles
- **Static spreadsheets** that provide no real-time optimization or prediction capability
- **Point solutions** that handle one aspect (e.g., berth planning) but don't integrate prediction, optimization, and workforce planning into a unified workflow

No existing solution provides a lightweight, integrated platform that combines **congestion prediction**, **CP-SAT optimization**, **what-if scenario comparison**, and **shift planning** in a single accessible interface.
